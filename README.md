# 🐾 PickPaw – Pet Adoption & Veterinary Management Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

**PickPaw** is a Pet Adoption and Veterinary Management Platform designed to simplify and streamline the pet adoption process. The system provides a centralized platform for managing pet profiles, adoption applications, user accounts, appointment scheduling, and adoption records.

It supports multiple user roles, including Admin, Staff, and Adopters, with role-based access to relevant features. The platform also helps maintain organized adoption records and generate useful statistics, such as adoption rates and popular pet breeds.

PickPaw aims to make pet adoption more accessible, improve shelter management efficiency, and help pets find loving homes.

## ✨ Features

### 👥 User Management
- User registration and login.
- User profile management.
- Role-based access control for Admin, Staff, and Adopters.

### 🐶 Pet Management
- Create and manage detailed pet profiles.
- Store pet information, including species, breed, age, health status, and pictures.
- Track pet availability using statuses such as Available, Reserved, and Adopted.

### 📝 Adoption Application Management
- Submit adoption applications with personal details and adoption preferences.
- Track application statuses: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
- Manage and review adoption requests.

### 📅 Appointment Scheduling
- Schedule appointments to visit the shelter and meet available pets.
- Organize visits to support a smoother adoption experience.

### 📋 Adoption Record Keeping
- Maintain a history of completed adoptions.
- Store details of adopted pets and their adopters.

### 📊 Adoption Statistics & Reports
- Generate reports on adoption rates.
- Identify popular pet breeds.
- Monitor adoption activities to support better shelter management.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify the pet adoption process.
- Improve the organization of pet and adopter information.
- Streamline adoption application processing and appointment scheduling.
- Maintain accurate adoption histories.
- Support data-driven decisions through adoption statistics.

## 💡 Benefits

PickPaw provides a structured way to manage pet adoption activities, reduces manual record-keeping, improves information accessibility, and helps shelters coordinate their adoption processes more efficiently.
